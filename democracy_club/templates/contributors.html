These amazing individuals ensure that we have maximum information available for voters ahead of each election. Interested in pitching in? Here’s when and how you can help. ## Around scheduled elections Scheduled elections in the UK happen each year on the first Thursday in May. It is in the run-up to this event that we are in greatest need of assistance. The main event is the deadline for candidates to register to stand, roughly a month before polling day (this date is different each year — we will publish it on our blog and social media). This is when official candidate lists are published, usually in PDF format, on local council websites. We require help from volunteers to enter the names into the database – this is a fairly hectic process which usually takes around 48 hours. {#**[Learn more about official candidate lists.](#sopn)**#} Once this initial work is completed, the second task is to collect a photograph and contact details of every candidate standing in the election. Each candidate has their own profile in the database, and this is displayed to users of our flagship voter information website [WhoCanIVoteFor.co.uk](https://whocanivotefor.co.uk/). We’re always looking for more people to help with this – you’re welcome to focus on a particular political party or region. * Does each candidate profile have a good quality recent colour photo? * Do they have links to social media, or an email address? * Can we find an election address published by them for the current election? **[Learn more about the details we collect.](https://candidates.democracyclub.org.uk/volunteer/)** Once the election is over we collect the results: votes cast for each candidate in First-Past-the-Post elections, and turnout data for other electoral systems. This data can be added to the candidate database: simply navigate to the [relevant ballot](https://candidates.democracyclub.org.uk/elections/). ## Other times of year The candidate database covers all by-elections, so the process described above occurs in miniature each week. We are always grateful for contributors who can dedicate a little time to check and update the latest by-election candidates. In the months ahead of each election, we construct spreadsheets of local political party details for display on each candidate’s profile on WhoCanIVoteFor.co.uk. This is a task we are always looking for help on — [you can find the latest sheets here](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/0/folders/17rAxFSG0V6FBQR2Bxjc5txCQKztOJ5NY). ## Keep in touch Key dates and calls for help will be published on our [blog]({% url 'hermes_post_list' %}), social media, and [mailing list](https://mailinglist.democracyclub.org.uk/) ahead of each election. **[Join our mailing list](https://mailinglist.democracyclub.org.uk/)** {% endfilter %} {# @formatter:on #} {# fmt:on #} {% endblock page_content %}
